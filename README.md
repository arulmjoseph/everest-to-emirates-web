# Everest to Emirates

Create a Nepal-themed landing page for the "Everest to Emirates" (Nepal Utsav) cultural program and book launch event based on the attached wireframe PDF and visual assets.

Sections to include:
- Header with Logo, Navigation (About, Objectives, Activities, Book, Gallery, Sponsors), and CTAs (Participate, Become a Sponsor)
- Hero banner (with option for video or image backdrop) featuring "Everest to Emirates" with dual Nepal & UAE imagery, headline, tagline "Culture • Connection • Collaboration", and dual action CTAs
- About / Introduction section highlighting the cultural initiative and bilateral celebration
- Objectives section with 6 cards/icons (Culture, Connection, Collaboration, Business, Stories, Community)
- Activities section highlighting Cultural Program, Fashion Show, Nepali Food, Book Launch, Networking, and Memorable Evening
- Dedicated Book spotlight section for "Everest to Emirates" showcasing the 3D book cover and chapters (Geography, Heritage, Culture, Fashion, Music, Dance, Food, Legends, Achievers)
- Who Can Participate section (Entrepreneurs/Business, Media/Writers, Artists/Creators, Organizations/Leaders)
- Partnership / Sponsorship packages section
- Sponsor & Partner logos grid with partnership inquiry CTA
- Gallery with responsive layout
- Instagram feed preview with follow CTA
- Single lead capture form connected to the CTAs with fields:
  - Name
  - Email id
  - Participate or Not (Yes / No)
  - Sponsor or not (Yes / No)
  - Submit button
- Submissions should send email data to dcom@eim.ae
- Thank you page / confirmation flow
- Final CTA section ("Let's Bring Nepal & UAE Together")
- Footer with logo, navigation links, social links, and contact info (dcom@eim.ae)
